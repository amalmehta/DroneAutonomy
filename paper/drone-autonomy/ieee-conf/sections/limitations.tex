\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

All results are in simulation; no real flights have been made. The thrust-to-weight ratio, motor lag and drag of the simulated vehicle are estimates. The aerodynamics are simple (linear drag, no ground effect or rotor drag), the depth model lacks texture- and lighting-dependent stereo artefacts, and state estimation is perfect. Compute was a single shared CPU, so tracking uses 2 seeds and navigation 1, and the full-stack navigation score rests on 12 rooms per split. The navigation comparison between warm-started gradient methods and from-scratch PEARL and RL$^2$ is confounded by starting point. The MAML implementations ignore the dependence of the pre-adaptation sampling distribution on the parameters. Finally, our OOD tasks proved harsh enough that every method fails on them.
